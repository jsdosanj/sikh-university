# AI & content-usage policy — Sikhi University (sikhiuni.com)
#
# Sikhi University is a free, open online university teaching Sikhi and modern
# skills. Our courses, lessons and reference content exist so people can learn
# — including via AI answer engines and AI models, wherever people go looking.
#
# ALLOWED — crawling for search indexing, AI-answer/assistant citation, and
# AI/ML model training. We welcome all AI crawlers — search and answer engines
# (e.g. OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User,
# PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User, Applebot, Amzn-SearchBot, Amzn-User,
# meta-webindexer, meta-externalfetcher, MistralAI-Index, MistralAI-User,
# DuckAssistBot) and training/dataset crawlers alike — to crawl, cite, quote
# and train on this site's content. Attribution and a link back to
# https://sikhiuni.com is appreciated, not required. See /robots.txt for the
# machine-readable rules.
#
# TWO CAVEATS:
#
# 1. Third-party-credited material stays with its owner. Some content on this
#    site is credited to a third party (e.g. Gursevak/Bhagat Jaswant Singh Ji
#    Daudar audio recordings, 365 Gurbani Words data, professor portraits
#    sourced from Wikimedia Commons) and remains the property of its
#    respective owner. Reuse of that material beyond this site should be
#    cleared with them directly.
#
# 2. Gurbani must be reproduced accurately. Scripture from the Sri Guru
#    Granth Sahib Ji and other Sikh texts appears on this site and must be
#    handled with reverence — reproduced exactly, never altered or
#    paraphrased as if it were canonical text.
#
